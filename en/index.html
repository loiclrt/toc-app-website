The gesture

Three taps. Slowly.

When you lock the door or turn off the stove, you tap the circle three times.
toc keeps the record. You move on.

Mindful checking means giving one gesture your full attention, once. A different rhythm from the repeated checking many people with OCD know well.

  1. 1LookThe locked door, the stove turned off. Take a second to really see it.
  2. 2Tap slowlyThree times, without rushing. Each tap is deliberate.
  3. 3Move onThe check is done and recorded. Your attention can come back to your day.
